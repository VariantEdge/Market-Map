# Market Maps - Photonics context

## Theme overview

The photonics market map organizes the optical supply chain so investors can distinguish upstream materials and equipment from active devices, connectivity ICs, photonic integrated circuits, foundry platforms, modules, transport, and testing.

## Layer definitions

Layer 1 covers materials, substrates, and process equipment. Higher layers cover active photonic devices, electro-optical connectivity ICs, photonic integrated-circuit platforms and IP, foundry and process platforms, photonic packaging and modules, optical infrastructure, transport, and test and qualification. A layer identifies value-chain participation, not a complete product-level competitor set.

## Value chain

Economic value moves from specialized materials and fabrication tools through devices, integrated circuits, foundry processes, packaging, modules, systems, transport, and qualification. Investors should identify where a company owns differentiated technology, where it relies on qualified suppliers, and which downstream step converts capacity into shipped product and revenue.

## Bottlenecks

Common photonics chokepoints include specialized materials, wafer and epitaxy capacity, process yield, laser supply, packaging, customer qualification cycles, thermal management, and test capacity. A capacity announcement is not the same as qualified output, shipped product, or recognized revenue.

## Relevant companies

Market Maps app data lists companies by layer. Companies in the same layer may be peers, suppliers, customers, or participants with different product scopes. Competitive analysis must match the specific product, manufacturing process, qualification status, and end market.

## Key debates

AI data-center demand can benefit several layers simultaneously. The central investor questions are which layer captures economic value, where supply is constrained, how quickly new capacity becomes revenue, whether differentiation supports durable margins, and whether longer-dated architecture transitions reach production on the expected timeline.
