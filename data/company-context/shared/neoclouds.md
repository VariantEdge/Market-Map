# Market Maps - Neoclouds context

## Theme overview

The neocloud market map covers specialized providers of accelerated computing infrastructure for artificial intelligence and high-performance computing. These platforms combine data-center capacity, power, accelerators, networking, orchestration software, and customer support.

## Layer definitions

Layer 1 covers public AI Infrastructure & Neocloud Platforms. The current map includes Nebius Group, CoreWeave, and IREN and applies the same company-page, market-data, peer-comparison, earnings, and chat framework used across Market Maps.

## Value chain

Economic value moves from power and real estate through data-center construction, cooling, networking, accelerators, systems integration, cloud software, and contracted compute consumption. A provider creates value only when installed capacity becomes reliable, utilized, and revenue producing at returns above its cost of capital.

## Bottlenecks

Common bottlenecks include power availability, grid interconnection, construction schedules, cooling, networking, accelerator supply, financing, customer qualification, software reliability, and the lag between capital deployment and recognized revenue.

## Relevant companies

Market Maps currently includes Nebius Group (NBIS), CoreWeave (CRWV), and IREN (IREN). Placement in the same layer indicates shared exposure to neocloud economics and does not imply identical business models or direct competition across every workload.

## Key debates

The core investor debates are whether contracted demand is durable, how quickly capacity converts to revenue, whether utilization and pricing support attractive returns, how customer concentration changes risk, and whether rapid hardware cycles or financing costs erode the value of growth.
